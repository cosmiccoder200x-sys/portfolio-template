How this portfolio handles project enquiries and visitor analytics.

Project enquiries

When you submit the Hire Me form, you provide your name, email address, project category, approximate budget and project description. The information is used to review your enquiry, contact you and manage a potential project discussion. Do not submit passwords, payment information or confidential credentials.

Storage and service providers

Enquiries may be stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare Turnstile checks submissions for spam. If email delivery is configured, your enquiry is sent to the portfolio owner's inbox through the configured email provider. Personal enquiry details are not displayed in public analytics.

Visitor analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is used to understand page visits and performance. The site can also count aggregate actions, such as viewing Hire Me, exploring templates and submitting an enquiry. These conversion events do not intentionally record cookies, names, emails or IP addresses, and respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

Retention and deletion

Enquiries are kept only as long as reasonably needed for project discussions, support or applicable recordkeeping. Inactive enquiries should be reviewed and deleted periodically. You can request access, correction or deletion of enquiry information using the contact address below. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.
